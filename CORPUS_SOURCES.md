# Extension corpus sources

Two original synthetic teaching files were drafted with AI assistance for this assignment; no external texts or private records were used. They may be shared with the assignment. Reproduce with `build_extension.py`, which does not read tests, answer keys or results.

- grammar.txt: 576 unique sentences teaching agreement, pronouns and tense in varied contexts.
- spatial_relations.txt: 445 unique sentences teaching containment, vertical/horizontal location and direction.

These categories were chosen because the starter had zero scorable cases in each. Teaching includes ordinary vocabulary and underlying concepts shared with the public benchmark, but not copied test prompts, paired reference answers or minimally substituted test stories. The exact-prefix check is supplemented by reviewing examples; it is not a semantic guarantee. Files use UTF-8 text, so PDF extraction is not applicable.

Corpus addition changes vocabulary, sampling distribution, split membership and panels. Keep the split seed/procedure fixed, but do not claim the panels are identical across corpus experiments.
